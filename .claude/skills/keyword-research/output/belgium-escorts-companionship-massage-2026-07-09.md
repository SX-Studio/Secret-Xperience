## The opportunity in one paragraph

Our SEO value is almost entirely **local-commercial** (`escort {city}` style) and it
concentrates where we have listings. Right now that's **Antwerp escorts (8 live
listings)** — a page that can rank *and* convert today — plus Brussels escorts and a
thin spread of single-listing towns. Two structural gaps cap us: (1) **there is no
`/massage` category page or massage city pages at all**, yet we have 4 massage listings
stranded on `/search`; and (2) **companionship and massage aren't in the city×category
route system**, so those listings have no local landing page. The fastest wins are:
harden Antwerp escorts, build the missing massage surface, and lean into an
**underserved male-escort/gigolo niche** where we're unusually deep (7 male providers).

## Compliance note

All targeted terms are **legal, consenting-adult commercial** queries (companionship,
escort, erotic/sensual massage, private reception, gigolo). **No** terms implying
illegal, trafficked, underage, or non-consensual services were included. Borderline
modifiers to avoid in titles/meta: anything implying "cheap/street", coercion, or age —
keep messaging premium, verified, discreet.
